Research Interests

  • Memory-bound LLM inference. KV cache compression, quantization and transform coding for long-context and agentic workloads.
  • Conditional computation. Mixture-of-Experts and training-free routing that turn static redundancy in pretrained models into input-dependent sparsity — and the serving systems that realize it under real memory budgets.
  • Efficiency claims, verified. Whether reported efficiency gains survive controlled comparison, distribution shift, and deployment.
